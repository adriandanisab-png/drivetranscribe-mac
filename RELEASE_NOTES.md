First public beta of DriveTranscribe for Mac.

- Drop a file or a whole folder and get the transcript, fully offline
- Editor synced with the video: click a word to jump, fix text, find & replace
- Speaker labels you can rename
- One-click English subtitles from Romanian video
- SRT, VTT and TXT with Reels layouts (3 words, 1 word, subtitles), optional automatic export next to the video
- Romanian and English with correct ă â î ș ț

The beta isn't signed by Apple yet: the first time, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
Requires macOS 15+ on Apple silicon.

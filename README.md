# DriveTranscribe for Mac

Turn any video or audio into text and subtitles, right on your Mac. Transcription runs offline with Whisper large-v3: your media never leaves your Mac.

**[⬇ Download the latest beta](https://github.com/adriandanisab-png/drivetranscribe-mac/releases/latest/download/DriveTranscribe-beta.dmg)** · [Website](https://drivetranscribe2026.web.app/mac.html)

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

## Installing the beta

1. Open the downloaded file and drag **DriveTranscribe** into **Applications**.
2. Open it. The beta isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time.
3. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
4. On first use the app downloads the transcription model (about 630 MB, once).
